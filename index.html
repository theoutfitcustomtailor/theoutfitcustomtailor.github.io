A suit on a Phuket holiday: how long it takes and what to bring

Most garments at The Outfit Custom Tailor are ready in 24 to 48 hours over two to three fittings. This assumes you are in Phuket for those fittings. The shop is a bespoke tailor on Bang Tao Beach, and this guide is for travellers planning around a holiday.

How long does a suit take?

A suit usually takes one or two days of your trip. The time between appointments fills those days, not the sewing.

Bigger jobs run longer: a full-canvas jacket, or a cloth the shop does not carry. High season also runs tighter. Share your travel dates, and the shop will tell you what is realistic.

What happens at each fitting?

The first fitting is on an unfinished garment, the second checks the corrections, and collection confirms the finished piece.

Large changes are still possible at the first fitting. At the second, the tailor marks trouser length against the shoes you bring. At collection only small adjustments are still possible.

What should you bring with you?

Bring the shoes you will wear with the suit and a shirt you wear often, plus anything that shows how you like clothes to fit.

  • The shoes you will wear with the suit, since the heel decides the trouser length.
  • A shirt you wear often, so the sleeve can be matched to it.
  • A garment of yours that fits well.
  • A garment that fits badly, with what bothers you about it.
  • A photo of the look you want.

Can the tailor come to your hotel?

Yes, within Bangtao Beach, Cherngtalay, Laguna and Surin. Hotels and villas there are visited at no extra charge.

Fittings in Kamala are arranged on request. The first appointment usually works better at the shop when you want to handle a lot of cloth. Later ones can be at your hotel.
